Why we keep buying in Darlington

We're not a national fund picking towns off a spreadsheet. We walk these streets, we know which ones let quickly and which don't, and our own money goes into every project before a partner's does.

Darlington is where our largest completed project sits: Northgate, a tired town-centre building we bought for £110k and re-engineered into design-led studio suites — refinanced at £750k and producing £109k a year. A few streets away, Station Road went from a £115k purchase to a £320k asset earning £44k a year.

Both are fully let and managed by our own team. That's our case for Darlington — made in bricks, not brochures.
