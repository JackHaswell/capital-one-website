For Property Owners

Your property. Our problem.

Own a property that could work harder — or want to own one without the graft? We find, develop and manage property across Darlington, Teesside and Hartlepool. Use one service, or hand us the whole journey.

01

We Find

Tell us what you're looking for — budget, area, the kind of return you want — and we'll go and find it. The same local network that feeds our own pipeline works your brief: undervalued buildings, often before they reach the open market.

  • A written appraisal with real numbers — purchase, works, end value, expected income
  • Off-market and below-market opportunities across the North East
  • A straight "walk away" when a deal doesn't stack up — we only win when you do
02

We Develop

Full-scope delivery on a property you own: design, compliance, build. The same team that converts our own buildings — tired terraces into fully compliant, design-led HMOs and premium single lets — runs your project end to end.

  • HMO conversion, full refurbishment, or reconfiguration to raise the rent a building can earn
  • Planning, licensing and compliance handled — one point of contact throughout
  • Monthly reporting: photos, spend, timeline, milestones
03

We Manage

Through our dedicated management arm, Capital One Property Management, your asset stays let, maintained and performing — hands-off income with one point of contact from viewing to tenancy.

  • Lettings, tenant management, maintenance and compliance under one roof
  • HMO and single-let management across Darlington, Teesside and Hartlepool
  • Already own elsewhere? We'll take over management of what you have
