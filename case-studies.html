Northgate

Completed · Fully Let

Town Centre, Darlington

Northgate is our largest completed project and the clearest picture of how we work. We bought a tired town-centre building for £110,000 — the kind of property most investors walk straight past — because we could see what it could become and we knew the demand was there.

£460,000 of development re-engineered the building into design-led studio suites: proper layouts, quality finishes, built to let quickly and keep letting. This wasn't a cosmetic refresh — it was a full transformation, run by our own team from planning through to completion.

The finished asset was refinanced at £750,000 and produces £109,000 a year in rent — fully let, with lettings and management handled in-house. The building that nobody wanted is now one of Darlington's best-presented residential addresses.

Alderson Street

Completed · Fully Let

Teesside

Alderson Street was bought for £90,000 and converted into a fully compliant six-bedroom HMO — licensed, fire-safe and finished to a standard that keeps rooms let and tenants staying.

£108,000 of works produced a £260,000 asset letting at £41,000 a year. HMO conversion is detail work: licensing, room sizes, fire regulation, management. It's exactly the kind of project where doing it properly is the difference between an asset and a liability.
