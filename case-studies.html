Northgate

Completed · Fully Let

100–104 High Northgate, Darlington

Northgate is our largest completed project and the clearest picture of how we work. When we bought it for £110,000 — sourced off-market, direct from the vendor — it was a tired semi-commercial building: an empty shop on the ground floor, a flat on the top floor and a derelict basement. The kind of property most investors walk straight past — but we could see what it could become, and we knew the demand was there.

A total project cost of £460,000 — including finance, interest and fees — re-engineered the building into six design-led studios and six en-suite letting rooms, with each floor served by its own communal kitchen for the shared en-suite bedrooms.

This wasn't a cosmetic refresh — it was a full back-to-brick rebuild, run by our own team from planning through to completion. The basement floor was dug out and relaid, the ceiling height in the two front bedrooms was raised, the bay windows were rebuilt from the basement up, old stairwells were removed, new windows and doors fitted throughout, and the whole building was soundproofed and thermally upgraded — delivered to the standard of finish today's working professionals expect.

The finished asset was refinanced at £750,000 and produces £109,000 a year in rent — fully let, with lettings and management handled in-house by Capital One Property Management. The building that nobody wanted is now one of Darlington's best-presented residential addresses.
