Questions investors ask us

What's the minimum investment?+

It varies deal by deal. Book a short call and we'll talk through what's currently open and whether it fits what you're looking for.

How are returns paid?+

A fixed return is agreed in writing before you invest. It's paid when the project completes — typically at refinance or sale — alongside the repayment of your original capital.

What actually secures my investment?+

Your loan is documented in a formal agreement and secured against the property itself. We encourage you to have your own solicitor review everything before you commit.

How long is my capital deployed?+

For the length of the project — agreed up front, before you invest. You'll know the expected timeline and exit route from day one.

What are the risks?+

Property investment places your capital at risk and returns are not guaranteed. Projects can overrun and market values can move. We mitigate this by buying below market value, securing every loan against the asset and reporting monthly — but you should never invest money you cannot afford to commit.
