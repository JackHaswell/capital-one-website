Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Capital One Property Partners Ltd. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled securely and responsibly. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Data We Collect

  • Personal data: your name, email address and any information you choose to include when you contact us by email or book a consultation through Calendly.
  • Technical data: we do not run analytics or tracking tools on this website.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To arrange and manage consultations you book with us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed below.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Calendly — used to schedule consultations. Information you submit when booking is processed by Calendly under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this site are served by Google; when the site loads, your IP address is sent to Google to deliver them.
  • GitHub Pages — this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure or loss, and we retain correspondence only for as long as necessary.

6. Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own. If you book a consultation, Calendly's own cookie policy applies to the booking process on their platform.

7. Your Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@capitalonepropertypartners.com.

8. Legal Compliance

We comply with the UK GDPR and other applicable privacy laws. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Updates

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at info@capitalonepropertypartners.com.
