Who We Are

Developers with skin in the game

Capital One Property Partners is built by two operators — not middlemen. We buy underperforming residential and commercial buildings in high-demand locations, re-engineer them into premium rental assets, and hold or exit for profit.

Every project we offer to investment partners is one we've underwritten with our own capital and manage hands-on, end to end. The staircase you're standing on? We rebuilt it.

  • ✓ Specialists in HMO conversion, serviced accommodation and buy-to-lets
  • ✓ Deep local knowledge across Teesside, Darlington & Hartlepool
  • ✓ Fixed-return investment structures, secured against the asset
  • ✓ Monthly investor reporting — full transparency from acquisition to exit

What We Do

HMO Development
